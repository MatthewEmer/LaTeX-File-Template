\section{The Bibliography File}

\subsection{Entry Formatting}

Bib\LaTeX\ provides a variety of entry types, for simplicity's sake, I have listed them below, alongside a description of their use, and their required fields for Harvard references as seen in the tables below. This was produced in combination between \newcitation{biblatex_entries_chat} and \newcitation{biblatex_manual}.

\begin{newtable}{BibLaTeX .bib file entry formats (traditional media).}{|c|c|c|c|}
    \hline
    \textbf{Source Type} & \textbf{Entry Type} & \textbf{Required Fields} & \textbf{Optional Fields} \\
    \hline
    Book & \@@book & \makecell{title, author, date.} & \makecell{editor, edition, chapter, \\ pages, publisher, location, \\ isbn, doi, url, urldate.} \\\hline
    \makecell{Dictionary/Encyclopedia \\Entry} & \@@inreference & \makecell{author, booktitle, title, date.} & \makecell{editor, edition, chapter, \\ pages, publisher, location, \\ isbn, doi, url, urldate.} \\\hline
    Film/Movie & \@@movie & \makecell{title, author, date.} & \makecell{publisher, location, url, \\urldate.} \\\hline
    Interview & \@@misc & \makecell{author, date, howpublished.} & \makecell{title, organization, url, \\urldate.} \\\hline
    Letter & \@@letter & \makecell{author, date, howpublished.} & \makecell{title, organization, url, \\urldate.} \\\hline
    Manual/Documentation & \@@manual & \makecell{title, author, date.} & \makecell{version, url, urldate.} \\\hline
    News Article & \@@article or \@@online & \makecell{title, author, journaltitle, \\date.} & \makecell{organization, pages, url, \\urldate.} \\\hline
    Speech & \@@unpublished & \makecell{author, date, eventtitle.} & \makecell{title, venue, organization, \\url, urldate.} \\
    \hline 
\end{newtable}
